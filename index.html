01.About Me

Hello! I’m Josua Cabanting, a Computer Engineer and Technical Support Engineer with hands-on experience in network monitoring, infrastructure management, and cybersecurity solutions.

I work with NetGain Systems, Tenable, NinjaOne, and LiveAction to maintain visibility into IT environments and support their performance and security. My role involves real-time monitoring, incident response, and troubleshooting to detect network issues, vulnerabilities, and potential security threats early.

With a growing focus on cybersecurity, I use Tenable for vulnerability assessment and risk identification, alongside network monitoring solutions to detect anomalies and improve network resilience. Through continuous monitoring and analysis, I help organizations maintain system availability and strengthen their security posture.

I’m passionate about bridging network operations and cybersecurity, and I continuously develop my skills to support secure, stable, and high-performing IT infrastructures.
